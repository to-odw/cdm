## **About the workshop**

The Cambridge Digital Minds Workshop brings together researchers, policy experts and fellows for an intensive period of discussion and deliberation on concrete questions about digital minds.  
Unlike a typical academic conference, the workshop is built around interactive sessions, structured discussions and collaborative exercises rather than a programme of talks. It is meant to be action-guiding: where possible, sessions lead to tangible outputs, such as reports and recommendations, that can shape research and policy on digital minds going forward.  
Session topics for 2027 will be announced closer to the time. All fellows from the [Cambridge Digital Minds Fellowship 2027](https://digitalminds.cam/fellowship-2027.html) are invited.

## **What makes this workshop different**

### **Action-guiding**

Sessions aim to produce concrete proposals that can inform real decisions, from research priorities to policy recommendations.

### **Timely and adaptive**

We focus on questions that matter now, and on proposals that can adapt as the circumstances of AI development change.

### **World-leading insight**

A small group of the world’s leading thinkers on digital minds, brought together in Cambridge to share key perspectives.

### **Mixed participation**

Fellows work alongside experts. Some sessions may be expert-only, but collaboration across career stages is the default.
