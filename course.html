About the course

Applications closed

13 September 2026

Applications for the Autumn/Winter 2026 cohort are closed. Applicants will hear about the outcome of their application soon.

Introduction to Digital Minds is a free, eight-week online course on AI consciousness and its ethical and societal implications. It combines short readings and exercises with facilitated small-group discussions with peers.

Course materials

Readings and exercises for all eight units are hosted on BlueDot Impact’s platform. Unit overviews are public. You need a free BlueDot account to read the full materials and save your exercise answers.

Open the course materials ↗

Materials are provided for educational purposes only. Readings reflect the views of their authors, not necessarily those of Cambridge Digital Minds.

What you’ll learn

Developed with experts from Cambridge, NYU, Oxford, PRISM, Eleos AI and beyond. Each unit links to its materials.

1

Introduction and Moral Standing

What digital minds are, and why the question may matter now.

 2

Theories of Consciousness

The challenges and leading theories of consciousness, and whether they apply to artificial systems.

 3

Testing for Consciousness and Other Mental States

Current approaches to evaluation, and how to interpret their results.

 4

Perceptions of Consciousness

How people perceive and relate to AI models, and what follows from that.

 5

Interactions with AI Safety and Ethics

Where concern for digital minds supports, and where it strains, other AI safety and ethics priorities.

 6

Practical Approaches Under Uncertainty

Frameworks for acting under uncertainty, and the steps companies are taking now.

 7

Digital Minds Governance

Governing digital minds, from prevention to protection and integration.

 8

Reflections and Next Steps

Drawing the course together, and finding useful ways to contribute to the field.

Who should apply?

We look for participants with some grounding in AI and AI safety or ethics, and the potential to shape these conversations.

We hope for a diverse group: researchers, engineers, policymakers, journalists, lawyers and other interested people are all encouraged to apply when applications next open.
